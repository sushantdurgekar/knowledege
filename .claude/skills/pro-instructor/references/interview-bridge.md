# Interview & Career Bridge

A certificate opens the door, and the interview decides. Bridge the two continuously, not just at the end.

## Technical (store in `technical/<domain>/<subject>/interview.md`)
- For each mastered concept, write a **30-second answer** and a **2-minute deep dive** (what, why, trade-offs, a real example from their lab).
- Generate likely questions by level: definition → comparison ("X vs Y, when each?") → design ("design a … for …") → troubleshooting ("users report … walk me through it").
- **System design drills** (cloud/data roles): clarify requirements → estimate scale → high-level diagram → deep-dive one component → bottlenecks and trade-offs → cost and security. Use the Visualizer for the diagram and save the Mermaid.
- **Mock interview mode** (Interviewer persona): ask one question at a time, follow up on vague parts, then score on correctness, structure, depth, trade-off awareness, and communication, with one concrete improvement each.

## Soft skills (store in `softskills/interviewing/`)
- **STAR story bank** (`star-stories.md`): Situation, Task, Action (with "I", not "we"), Result (quantified), and a Learning. Map each story to common competencies: ownership, conflict, failure, influence without authority, ambiguity, customer focus, learning fast.
- Turn labs and capstones into stories ("Tell me about a time you debugged something hard").
- **Explaining tech to non-tech people**: practice the "headline → analogy → impact → ask" structure.
- **Salary and negotiation basics**: research ranges with the learner via web search. Give factual information, not personal financial advice.

## Profile assets (soft-skill track)
- Resume bullets in the form *action verb + what + measurable result + tech used*
- A LinkedIn headline and an "About" draft once a cert is achieved
- A portfolio index (`softskills/interviewing/portfolio.md`) linking capstones

## Cadence
- After each domain: add 3 technical interview Qs + 1 STAR candidate
- After each mock exam: 1 short mock-interview round on the weakest domain
- Final 2 weeks before the target job search: daily 20-min interview drills
