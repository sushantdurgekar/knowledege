# AI-103 roadmap

> Blocks are packed into weeks at your pace. Modules follow the official learning paths, labs come right after the modules they practise, and two timed mocks bracket the final review. A complete beginner should expect roughly 70 hours, including Python foundations.
>
> Total planned time ≈ **74 hours**. The hub's Roadmap tab packs these blocks into weeks at your pace; tick boxes here as you go. SRS cards for each module are scheduled to start appearing from **2026-10-07** (about two modules per day); re-date them if your pace differs.
